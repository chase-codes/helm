The order of service

Four tracks, in the order Sunday uses them.

Helm is built around the shape of a service rather than a pile of slide decks. Each track is a tab, and you move along as the morning moves.

Before the service

The pre-service loop

A welcome card, a verse, the announcements, prayer requests — rotating on a dwell timer while people find their seats. Tap any card to switch to it straight away. Verse cards pull their text from the installed Bible by reference, so nobody retypes Psalm 122 into a text box.

When the singing starts

Songs

Search the library by title or by a line you can only half remember. Enter cues the verse, Enter again puts it on the screen, and the arrow keys walk the stanzas — so the whole song can be run without looking down at the keyboard.

I once was lost, but now am found,
Was blind, but now I see.

Searched on “was blind” · Amazing Grace, verse 1

When the text is read

Scripture

Type john 3:16 and press Enter. The verse is on the wall before the preacher finishes saying it, with the rest of the chapter in a rail alongside for when he keeps reading. KJV is installed out of the box; add more translations to compare them side by side.

Through the preaching

The Message

Sermon tapes with full-text search that works offline — find a tape, find a quote by the phrase you remember, or search inside the tape you're already in. Put any paragraph on the screen as a quote slide. Transcripts the church already has can be imported from .txt or .pdf.

Searched offline, quoted by paragraph, on the screen in two keystrokes.

Message track · tape, quote, and within-tape search

  • No internet on Sunday

    Search, cueing, and projection all run locally. A dropped connection in the building doesn't touch the service.

  • Your library, your machine

    Songs, Bibles, and sermons live in a database file on your own disk. No account, no subscription, nothing to sign into.

  • Plug in whenever

    Connect the projector before or after launching — the output window finds the second display and attaches itself.

Download the installer

On first launch Windows will show “Windows protected your PC”. Choose More info, then Run anyway. Helm installs for your user only — no admin password needed.

macOS

Apple silicon · 291 MB

Download the .dmg

On first launch macOS will say Helm is from an unidentified developer. Right-click Helm in Applications, choose Open, then confirm. You only do this once.

Updates Download the new .dmg from here. Self-updating on macOS needs the signing certificate too.
